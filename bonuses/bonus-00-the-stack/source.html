The Bonus Stack

Five printable tools designed to live alongside the app — one for every stage of the budgeting cycle. Use them in order the first month. Cherry-pick after that, as the situation calls for them.

Five bonuses · sixteen pages Print as needed

How the stack works

The app handles the math: where you stand, what's coming due, what's left between paydays. The stack handles everything around the math — the routine that makes you check the app at all, the mindset that lets you spend what's yours, the recovery rules for when things get hard.

Each bonus is self-contained. Each one is printable. Each one has a small artifact at its heart — a checklist, a worksheet, a wallet card — that you can reuse without re-reading the framing pages.

Recommended first month

On your first payday after starting the app, run Bonus 01. That same day, read Bonus 02 and fill in the worksheet. On day two, set up whichever savings track from Bonus 03 fits your pay schedule. Keep Bonus 04 nearby for the first tempting purchase. Save Bonus 05 for if and when you need it.
