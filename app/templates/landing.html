Common questions

Things people ask before signing up.

Do I need to connect my bank account?

No. Payday Tracker is manual by design — you enter what you want to track. We never see your bank, your debit card, or your transactions. Some people prefer it that way; the awareness IS the product.

How much does it cost?

Free during beta. We'll add a paid tier later for advanced features (charts, household accounts, deeper history). Beta users get a permanent founder discount on whatever we charge.

I'm paid monthly. Does this still work?

Yes. Weekly, biweekly, and monthly all work — pick when you sign up. The pay-period framing is the same either way; the math just runs on your schedule.

How is this different from YNAB, Mint, or Monarch?

Those are built for salaried workers paid monthly, with budgets defined in calendar months. Payday Tracker is built around your actual pay period — the two weeks between deposits — because that's the unit of time that matters when money is tight.

Can I use this with a partner?

Right now, individual accounts only. Joint and household accounts are on the roadmap but not yet available.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You can delete your account anytime from settings, and your data goes with you. You can also export everything as JSON before deleting. No lock-in.

Will my data be secure?

Passwords are hashed with scrypt; sessions use signed tokens. We never store payment info — that's handled by Stripe when paid plans launch. We're a small operation; no ads, no data resale.
